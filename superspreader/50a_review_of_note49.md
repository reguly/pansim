## Errors and unsupported assumptions in note 49

1. **L is a venue multiplier on the infection ratio of everyone present, so it acts on infectiousness and on susceptibility together (R8-0 item 1).** The ratio of a venue is `1 - exp(-k (sum I / N^alpha) L boost seasonality dt)` and is applied to every susceptible agent present. An agent who spends hours at an L = lambda venue infects lambda times as many people per hour and is exposed lambda times as much per hour. The two sides cannot be separated by changing L, so the class is fully coupled at the high-L venues, and received infections of the class rise with offspring (the round-7 coupling finding does not carry over). I did not implement a per-agent infectiousness-only multiplier, because that is the trait route of rounds 5 and 6.
2. **How L is changed.** Copies of `locations1.json` with `infectious` multiplied by lambda on types 5, 6, 9, 10 (659 venues: 420, 20, 52, 167). `--venueProfile covid` has fixed factors (5: 4, 6: 3, 9: 3, 10: 0.3) and `--communityScale` multiplies all community venues, so neither can do this. Public space (type 1) and type 15 keep L = 0.
3. **The proposed high-L set already holds most of everyone's discretionary hours (R8-0 item 2).** From the movement logs (2 weeks, role-based discretionary hours, `50_r80_hours_by_type.csv`): others spend 53.5% of their 3.40 discretionary hours per day at types 5, 6, 9, 10 (1.87 hours per day, all roles); the S2 class (f = 5%, F = 3) spends 59.1% of 9.88 hours (5.90 hours per day). Hours at these types in a non-discretionary role (work, school) are only 0.05 hours per day, so L by physical type is close to L by role for this set. Consequences: the untilted class already has a high-L hours ratio of 3.2, and tau = 1 can raise it only to 11.0 hours per day (ratio 5.9), not by a factor of 1/0.59 on a small base. The note's "required lambda 14 for r = 12 if others' time at high-L venues is negligible" cannot hold with others at 1.87 hours; in S8-3 others fall to 0.45 hours per day (0.2 scaling in windows with a home event), so the high-L hours ratio is 24.8.
4. **"Tau" is ambiguous.** Implemented as the probability that a low-L discretionary visit of a class member (types 1, 7, 8) is replaced, at the same duration, by a visit to a high-L type the agent has (uniform over the available types among 5, 6, 9, 10, then a uniform venue of that type). This keeps class hours and visit counts exactly, which a change of chances inside windows does not (windows are renewal processes and durations differ by type; round 7, D1). Realised class hours at high-L types: tau = 0.5: 8.46 hours per day (80.6% of discretionary hours); tau = 1: 11.03 hours per day (99.96%), against 5.90 untilted.
5. **S8-3 cannot preserve others' hours** (it moves them to home chances, as the note says), which conflicts with the general rule that hours are preserved. Others' discretionary hours fall from 3.40 to 2.13 per day (-37%) and the population mean from 3.50 to 2.45 (-30%); only windows with a home event change. This is reported as a deviation, not corrected.
6. **The predictor of R8-0 item 3 needs a productivity per hour by venue type, and the unscaled part is not worth a venue hour.** With all hours weighted equally the predicted class ratio at L = 1 is 1.0, against 1.55 observed (S2 cell). I derived the relative offspring weight per hour at each physical type from the baseline infection shares by type (round-7 S1, shape 3) and the baseline movement log (`50_r80_productivity.csv`): a home hour is 1, school 5.1, venues of types 5, 6, 9, 10 about 4.2 to 4.6, shops 3.9 to 4.3. Weight of an agent = sum over types of productivity x L x hours, with infectious time in N, M and S (25.6%) spent at home unless the agent breaks isolation. Out of sample check at L = 1: predicted class ratio r = 1.40 against 1.55 observed, predicted k 4.44 against 4.25 observed. CV^2 is taken relative to the baseline weights, so that the baseline gives k = 4.59.
7. **The skip rule and the stop rule conflict.** "Skip any arm whose predicted k is above 3" would skip S8-1 and S8-4 at every lambda, but the order of work asks to run S8-1 and S8-4 and report predictions that concern relative changes (k above 2.5; k changes by less than 10%). My reading: the arms that gate the decision (S8-1 and S8-2 at lambda 10, S8-4 cells) are run and reported with their predicted k and a note that the skip rule would have excluded them; S8-1 at lambda 3 and 30, and S8-2 at lambda 3, are skipped by the rule (predicted k 4.1 to 6.6) unless S8-2 at lambda 10 gives k below 2.
8. **S8-4 leaves the class factor and the lambda 10 comparison unspecified.** The class (5% and 20%) has no hours multiplier (F = 1), only the W schedule in N and M. "lambda 10 is below S8-2 tau 1 at the same lambda" is read as: the k of the S8-4 lambda 10 cell is above the k of S8-2 tau 1 lambda 10 (isolation breaking alone raises class weight less than tilting). If the intended meaning is the reverse, say so.
9. **S8-6.** `--hviMode` cannot be combined with `--sheddingShape` only because of a check in `initializeArgs`; the two act on different quantities (movement, infectiousness per episode). Removing the check for `--sheddingShape` is a one-line change, but I will decide after the main arms.
10. **S8-5, "cap the 5% most active".** `capTop` sets the class multiplier to 1 (hours back to the population mean), but the class keeps its tilt (venue choice), as the note defines the cap on activity. The other two interventions are as in round 7.

## What was implemented (all options default off, hazard kernel unchanged)

- `--hviTilt tau`, `--hviHighTypes` (default 5,6,9,10): substitution of venue type at fixed duration for class members in states W and N.
- `--hviOthersHighScale s`: for agents outside the class, in windows that have a home event, chances of the high-L types are multiplied by s and the difference is added to the home chance.
- `--hviIsolationBreak 1`: class members use their W schedule in states N and M.
- Mobility log: hours by physical venue type for every role (`hpt_*`).
- All new options need `--hviMode class` (error otherwise). T1: with every option off, a 12-week run on the new binary (`build_r8`) gives a daily table and an infection trace byte-identical to `build_r7b` (81,610 trace lines; the stdout differs only in the timing profile lines).
