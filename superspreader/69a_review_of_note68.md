I did not upload them again. The Drive connector only takes the file content as text in the call, so I could not upload them myself in round 13; for round 14 I upload every per-replicate file through the connector in pieces of at most about 70 KB, split by cell, and say so in the upload index.
2. **Cost.** At about 10 s of wall clock per replicate with 12 runs at once (memory about 0.9 GB per run limits the concurrency to 12), run A is about 55 minutes (six calibrations of 3 to 6 iterations of 12 replicates), B1 about 60 minutes (three worlds, up to four trials of up to four calibration iterations of 6 replicates, then 12 replicates), B2 about 25 minutes (no day-28 calibration, so one batch of 6 replicates per trial). Run A and B1 run together (six processes with two runs each), then B2: about 2 hours in all, not 6.
3. **B1 stopping rule.** A trial (6 replicates) has a standard error of about 0.02 in likelihood k, so a trial stops at |k - 0.27| of at most 0.02; the final 12-replicate cell is accepted at 0.24 to 0.30. If the final cell falls outside, I correct the parameter once or twice (log-log slope from the trials) and rerun the 12 replicates; these corrections go beyond "four trials" and are reported as such.
4. **B2 "alternating trials".** I update both quantities in every trial (coefficient from the ratio of the mean offspring to 0.88 with an exponent measured between trials, starting at 0.9; the tuned parameter from the log-log secant of likelihood k), which converges at least as fast as alternating. The starting point is the B1 final cell: coefficient times 0.65 for worlds 1 and 2b, times 0.5 for world 3b; the coefficient is not allowed below 3% of its B1 value. Accept at |R/0.88 - 1| at most 0.02 and |k - 0.27| at most 0.02 on 6 replicates; at most five trials; then the 12-replicate final, with up to two corrections if it misses the 3% and 0.24 to 0.30 windows.
5. **World 3b in B2 is probably unreachable.** At the B1 event rate (about 0.17) the event channel alone gives 0.64 times the early R of 1.48 at rate 0.1456, so about 1.0 offspring per index case at the round-13 coefficient; a mean of 0.88 needs a lower event rate, which raises k (rate 0.0966 gives k 0.52). The tuning will show where this ends; I report the closest cell and why, as the note allows.
6. **Index cases of at least 8,000 in B2.** There is no command-line option for the number of initial infections (the 358 infected agents of day 0 come from the states in the agent file, and the file must not be edited), so I use the note's other option: if the 12 final replicates have fewer than 8,000 index cases I run 12 more replicates (seeds 77013 to 77024) with the same parameters and report 24.
7. **Prediction run A (e)** is a screen over about 30 statistics in six cells, so at 3 combined standard errors about 0.5 exceedances are expected by chance (a two-set difference has one degree of freedom, heavier tails than the normal): I report the number of exceedances, not only whether there is one.
8. **Seeds.** Seeds 78001 to 78012 are the N1 set 78 used for the round-12 settings of the same worlds; the run-A cells are therefore paired by seed with those N1 cells (not an independent set from them, but independent of the round-13 N3 cells on 77001 to 77012).
