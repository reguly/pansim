## Errors and corrections to note 46

1. **Durations are hh.mm numbers, not decimal hours.** `TimeDay(double)` converts the fractional part as minutes: `minutes = round(fraction / 0.6 * 60)`, so 0.3 is 30 minutes, 1.3 is 1 hour 30 minutes. The discretionary durations in `agentTypes_3.json` are: type 1 (public space) 0.3 = 30 min, type 7 (short visit) 0.3 = 30 min, type 8 (long visit) 1 h, types 5, 6 and 10 are 2 h, type 9 (church) 1.3 = 90 min. The baseline file `46_baseline_discretionary_mobility_from_json.csv` used 0.3 h and 1.3 h, so it understates the hours of every visit to types 1 and 7 (by two thirds) and type 9 (by 15%), and the mean visit length. Baseline hours will be measured, not derived (`47_r70_baseline_mobility.csv`).
2. **The stated chances are not visit counts.** A window is not decided once. When a visit ends while the clock is still inside the window, the agent draws again from the same window, so the number of visits depends on the durations (a longer visit means fewer redraws). Time between windows is also assigned: if the next window starts less than 30 minutes away the agent stays where it is, between 30 and 60 minutes it goes to a public-space venue (type 1), and beyond 60 minutes it goes home. So public-space time arises outside the schedule's own type-1 events, and shortening a discretionary stay moves time to home or public space. This is why the baseline has to be measured on the running model.
3. **Hours are not proportional to a duration multiplier.** Because the window is a renewal process, the discretionary share of a window is m a / (m a + b) for a multiplier m on the discretionary durations (a and b the baseline discretionary and other chance-weighted durations), which saturates, and the next window's end truncates a stay. A mean-1 multiplier therefore gives mean realised hours below baseline. T3 and T4 will report this; the multiplier is rescaled by agent type (`--hviScaleByType`) on movement-only runs to bring the mean back within 2%.
4. **L is 1 for every venue type except public space and the commuter box.** In `locations1.json` and `locations3.json` the `infectious` field is 1.0 for all places of types 2 to 14, 22, 33 and 44, and 0.0 for type 1 (public space, 4,103 places) and type 15. With `--venueProfile off`, which every study run uses, "L-weighted hours" are therefore hours at venues other than public space. The type-mix lever (4.2, item 4) has nothing to act on except public space against the rest, and I do not implement it (departure D5 below).
5. **Window sums.** Confirmed: 9 of 363 windows do not sum to 1 (2.5%): agent type 2 W weekday 18 to 19 (0.85), type 4 W weekday 7 to 8 (0.60), type 4 N weekday 17 to 18 (0.95), type 4 W weekend 21 to 24 (0.90), type 5 N weekend 19 to 21 (1.05), type 6 N weekend 14 to 16 (1.45), type 7 N weekday 10 to 12 (0.95), type 8 N weekday and weekend 10 to 12 (1.05). If the sum is below 1, the remainder goes to the last event of the window (the loop overruns and steps back one); if it is above 1, the events past the point where the cumulative chance exceeds 1 are never chosen. The overrun reads one event past the window, which is harmless except at the end of the array.
6. **W, N, M, S.** From `transition_config.json`: W is S (susceptible), E, I1, I2 and R; N is I3; M is I4; S is I5_h, I6_h and R_h; D is dead. So the infectious time in W covers I1 and I2 (infectiousness 1 and 0.75), N is I3 (1), M is I4 (1) and S is hospital (0.1). Main arms apply the multiplier in W and N as the note says. Whether the infectious share in N and M is large will be reported as 4.3 asks.
7. **Repertoire.** `agents2.json`: every agent has exactly 1 public space, 3 type-5, 3 type-6, 3 type-9 and 3 type-10 venues; type 7 has 3 (2 for the stay-at-home type), type 8 has 4 to 6 (mean 4.5 for full-time workers, 3.5 for stay-at-home). `47_r70_repertoire.csv` has all types for both files.
8. **Time step.** 10 minutes. A stay shorter than 10 minutes rounds down to zero steps; the implementation sets a minimum of one step.

## Implementation, and departures from the guidelines (before running)

All new options default off and act only through the movement policy; the infection kernel is unchanged.

- D1. **Hours lever.** The multiplier scales the duration of each discretionary stay (types 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 10) picked by the schedule, in the well-being states set by `--hviStates` (default W and N), for eligible agents (age 15 and over, agent types 4 to 8, as the note suggests). I do not add extra excursions or change home chances, because the window logic already redraws and it keeps the schedule structure; the waking-day cap is the end of the window, and its effect is measured, not imposed.
- D2. **Mean preservation** through `--hviScaleByType` on movement-only runs (T3), to within 2% by agent type on weekdays and weekends where one scale allows; otherwise the deviation is reported.
- D3. **Visits lever.** Class members split each discretionary stay into V shorter stays at different venues of the same type, using a per-agent counter that forces the next V - 1 decisions; hours are unchanged apart from window slack. Integer V.
- D4. **Repertoire lever.** A new agents file with enlarged venue lists for the HVI class, built from the HVI assignment of a fixed `--hviSeed` (the same assignment in every replicate), because per-replicate agent files are impractical. The other arms use the run seed.
- D5. **Type-mix lever not implemented** (error 4).
- D6. **Occupational-mobility cell.** 1% of full-time workers (agent type 6) spend the 8-hour working day as 1-hour visits to a rotating set, their own venues of types 5, 7 and 8 (about 10.5 venues), instead of their workplace.
- D7. **Control (S6).** Implemented by changing the multipliers from day 21 (`--hviControlMode capTop` or `scaleAll`) and, for closure, with the existing closure rules.
- D8. **Own stream.** The HVI draw uses its own generator, seeded from the run seed (or `--hviSeed`) and the agent id, as the trait does. `--hviMode` with `--traitShape` or `--sheddingShape` is an error.
- D9. **Instrumentation.** `--mobilityLog` (per agent: moves, hours by place group, distinct discretionary venues per day and per week, hours in each well-being state, infectious hours by state) and `--hviDump` (multiplier, class, rotating flag). The infector and infectee multipliers are joined from the dump and the infection trace in the analysis, so no new trace column is added.
